**Phased Development Plan**

**Phase 1: Months 1 \- 2**

**Client Onboarding \+ management efficiencies (potentially staff training)**

Confirmed as the highest-priority, fastest-to-implement work. Steve validated that onboarding is the most time-consuming manual process. Documented processes and data already exist. Both can be scoped and built concurrently for immediate profitability improvement.

• **Onboarding:** AI-powered questionnaire processing, document generation, CRM setup guidance, central client knowledge hub

• **Offboarding:** Automated survey handling, retention workflows, standardised responses

• **Outcome:** Faster client setup, freed capacity per team member, better client experience from day one

**Phase 2: Months 2 \- 3+**

**Client Comms AI ("Steve AI") \- MVP**

**MVP:** AI handles simple, repeatable email responses the team already knows the answers to. Immediate time saving across the team.

**Library Build:** Identify and catalogue complex/unique requests. When they arise, the team queries Steve AI which references prior cases and decisions (citing precedent). Human reviews and sends.

**Cultural Shift:** In parallel, define the "black and white" standards the business wants to enforce. Embed these into the AI so it becomes the enforcement mechanism rather than relying on individual judgment.

Bigger data collection exercise but delivers the highest impact on profitability. Requires email export, call transcription, and theme categorisation. Built iteratively with team adoption and testing throughout.

**Phase 3: Future**

**Productised AI Tools & AI as a Service**

The strategic scale play. Three sub-opportunities that build on Phases 1-2:

• **Internal AI Product:** Account managers use AI to manage 30-50 clients each instead of 22-23. Proven internally before going external.

• **External AI Product:** Productised gym marketing AI taken to market. 100,000+ gyms addressable. White-label for other agencies.

• **AI as a Service:** Offer AI automation directly to gym clients (voice AI for payments, chatbots, etc.) at a margin. Near-term revenue using existing trust.
